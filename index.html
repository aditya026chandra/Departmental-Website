About the Department

Welcome to the Department of Commerce at Sri Venkateswara College, University of Delhi. The department aims to provide quality commerce education, analytical skills, and practical exposure to students in the fields of finance, marketing, accounting, and business management.

Activities & Societies

The Commerce Association is the official umbrella society of the Department of Commerce. It organizes all departmental events, speaker sessions, workshops, and academic competitions throughout the year.

  1. Academic Seminars and Guest Lectures
  2. Skill-building Workshops
  3. Inter-college Commerce Competitions and Annual Fest
